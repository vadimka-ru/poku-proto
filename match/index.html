Six fighters, one arena
Godot client (GDScript) + the real simulation in WebAssembly, now with the HUD and controls: you play seat 0, the other five are bots. Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and shoots, SUPER and emote buttons. Turn the phone to landscape before you tap. First load is about 12 MB.
