Poku: match preview

Six bots, one arena
Godot client (GDScript) + the real simulation in WebAssembly. No controls or HUD yet: you watch a bot match and read the numbers in the top-left corner. Turn the phone to landscape before you tap. First load is about 12 MB.

What to read off the screen

Top-left overlay: FPS, mean and p99 frame time over the last 300 frames, bridge ms per frame (simulation step plus snapshot), dropped ticks, platform. Screenshot it after 30 seconds of play. Add &perf=0 to a link to hide it, &seed=N or &seat=0..5 to change the match.
