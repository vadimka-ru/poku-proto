Poku: match

You and five bots, one arena
Godot client (GDScript) with the real simulation in WebAssembly. Turn the phone to landscape before you tap. Left thumb moves, right thumb shoots (tap = one shot at the nearest enemy, hold = keep shooting, drag = aim by hand), the lightning button is your super, the round portrait in the corner sends an emote, pause is Telegram's Back arrow (the bars in the corner outside Telegram). First load is about 12 MB.

On a computer

Keyboard and mouse: WASD / arrows move, left button shoots at the cursor, right button (hold and release) is the super, 1-5 emotes, Esc pauses. Add &touch=1 to a link to get the phone controls with the mouse.

Read-outs

Top-left overlay (on in these links): FPS, mean and p99 frame time over the last 300 frames, bridge ms per frame, dropped ticks, platform. Tap the very top-left corner to hide or show it. Links accept &perf=0, &seed=N, &seat=0..5, &haptics=1 (Telegram vibration on your shots, super and death).
