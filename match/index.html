Poku: match

Break the base: one on one
Godot client (GDScript) with the real simulation in WebAssembly. Turn the phone to landscape before you tap. Two Pokus, one forest, one base each: break the other base, best of three rounds. Left thumb moves, right thumb shoots (tap = one shot at the nearest enemy, hold = keep shooting, drag = aim by hand), the lightning button is your super, pause is Telegram's Back arrow (the bars in the corner outside Telegram). Invite a friend with a link from FRIENDS; a match against a bot is for testing.
Start Match against a bot

On a computer

Keyboard and mouse: WASD / arrows move, left button shoots at the cursor, right button (hold and release) is the super, 1-5 emotes, Esc pauses. Add &touch=1 to a link to get the phone controls with the mouse.

Read-outs

Top-left overlay (on in these links): FPS, mean and p99 frame time over the last 300 frames, bridge ms per frame, dropped ticks, platform. Tap the very top-left corner to hide or show it. Links accept &perf=0, &seed=N, &seat=0..5, &haptics=1 (Telegram vibration on your shots, super and death).
